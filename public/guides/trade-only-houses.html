Trade-Only Houses in Adopt Me

By AdoptMeHouseTrading.com · Updated October 2026 · 2 min read

In this guide

  1. What trade-only means
  2. The Last Chance House Sale, June 2024
  3. The final-chance Robux sale, April 2026
  4. A house that ended with an event
  5. Common questions

What trade-only means

A trade-only house is one the game no longer sells. You cannot buy it in the shop or with Robux anymore. The only way to own one is to trade with a player who already has it.

Adopt Me added house trading on May 31, 2024, and it follows its own rules. The how to trade houses guide covers them: a trade license, at least two houses, and one house per trade.

The Last Chance House Sale, June 2024

Seven houses went on sale at a discount and were then removed one a day:

  • Mansion, removed on June 8, 2024
  • Cozy Cabin, removed on June 9, 2024
  • Biodome Home, removed on June 10, 2024
  • Ruler's Castle, removed on June 11, 2024
  • Pirate Ship House, removed on June 12, 2024
  • Eco Natural Earth House, removed on June 13, 2024
  • Mermaid Mansion, removed on June 14, 2024

The final-chance Robux sale, April 2026

On April 24, 2026, Adopt Me ran a final-chance sale of Robux items before taking them away. Five house items were in it: the Modern Mansion, the Celebrity Mansion, the Soccer Stadium, the School and Hospital bundle (which covers two houses) and the Fossil Isle Returns Bundle (which includes the Expandable Dino House).

That makes six houses that are no longer sold: Celebrity Mansion, Expandable Dino House, Hospital Home, Modern Mansion, Schoolhouse Home, Soccer Stadium. Adopt Me's own post does not give a removal date, so this guide does not either.

A house that ended with an event

The Christmas Pudding House was a free reward for finishing 15 pet needs in the Winter Event. The event started on December 5, 2025, and the house left the game on January 5, 2026. It could not be traded at first, and became tradable on January 13, 2026.

Common questions

Can I still get one?

Yes, by trading with a player who owns it. Look at the live listings on this site, or list that you are looking for one.

Will a retired house come back?

We have not seen any report of one of these houses returning to sale, but nobody outside Adopt Me can say what comes next.

Can a Robux house be traded?

Yes. A house that was bought with Robux can be traded to another player. It just cannot be sold for Bucks.

Dates come from Adopt Me's own update posts and patch notes, and from the Adopt Me Wiki. Checked October 30, 2026. The game can change these, so check in game before you plan a trade around them.
