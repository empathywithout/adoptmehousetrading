What expandable means

Most Adopt Me houses are a fixed size. An expandable house starts small and lets you add extra rooms and floors, which you buy separately with Bucks. Adopt Me added the first three on August 31, 2023, with a furniture pack for each, and said that future houses may come with the feature. It was not added to the older houses.

Each expansion shows from the outside, except the basement. Four houses in the game are expandable. Their prices in Bucks are below.

Expandable Friendly Home

The Expandable Friendly Home was bought with Bucks. It starts with 1 floor. The four expansions cost 1,200 Bucks in all.

Expandable Dino House

The Expandable Dino House came with the Fossil Isle Returns Bundle, which is no longer sold. It starts with 1 floor. The four expansions cost 2,600 Bucks in all.

Things to keep in mind

  • Expansion prices are in Bucks, even for the houses that were bought with Robux.
  • The house price and the expansions are separate. Add them up before you decide.
  • If you trade an expandable house, look at which expansions it has. They show from the outside, so ask for photos of the inside and outside.

Expansion prices come from Adopt Me's Expandable Houses update notes (August 31, 2023) and the Adopt Me Wiki. Checked October 30, 2026. The game can change these, so check in game before you plan a trade around them.
