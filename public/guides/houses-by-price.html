Houses bought with Bucks

These are the houses with a Bucks price, from cheapest to most expensive. The Tiny Home is the free house every new player starts with, and extra copies cost 240 Bucks. After that, the cheapest is the Family Home at 489 Bucks, and the most expensive is the Monster Mansion at 13,000 Bucks.

Houses marked "Event house" came out in an event or update. Nobody has confirmed whether they are still sold, so check in game before you count on buying one.

Houses bought with Robux

4 houses are still listed as Robux purchases: Expandable Luxury Mansion, Millionaire Mansion, Space Fleet House, Toyshop House. Sources disagree on the current Robux prices for several of them, so this guide does not list a price. Check the price in the game's shop.

A house bought with Robux cannot be sold for Bucks, even if you got it in a trade.

Houses you can only get by trading

14 houses are no longer sold at all: Biodome Home, Celebrity Mansion, Christmas Pudding House, Cozy Cabin, Eco Natural Earth House, Expandable Dino House, Hospital Home, Mansion, Mermaid Mansion, Modern Mansion, Pirate Ship House, Ruler's Castle, Schoolhouse Home, Soccer Stadium. The only way to get one is to trade with another player who owns it. The trade-only houses guide explains when each one left.

A few things to know about the prices

  • Prices are what Adopt Me charged when we last checked. Updates can change them.
  • A price covers the house, not the furniture. Some houses come with furniture and some come empty. For example, the Monster Mansion has ten matching furniture pieces that are sold separately.
  • What a house costs in the shop is not what it trades for between players. Traders agree on their own prices, and a house with a great build can ask for more. See how the price gets decided.

Prices come from Adopt Me's update notes and the Adopt Me Wiki. Checked October 30, 2026. The game can change these, so check in game before you plan a trade around them.
