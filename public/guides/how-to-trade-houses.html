How to Trade Houses in Adopt Me

By AdoptMeHouseTrading.com · Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

In this guide

  1. How house trading works
  2. Which houses you can still get
  3. How the price gets decided
  4. Tips for a safer trade
  5. Red flags to watch out for

How House Trading Works

Adopt Me added house trading on May 31, 2024. It works differently from trading a pet, so it helps to know the steps before your first trade. These are the official rules from Adopt Me's own help center.

  • You need a trade license to use house trading.
  • You need to own at least two houses. After the trade, the game switches you to another house you own.
  • Only one house can be in a trade.
  • You can trade a house for anything that can normally go in a trade, like pets, toys, food, vehicles and pet wear. The one thing you cannot trade a house for is another house.
  • The VIP house cannot be traded.
  • A house that was bought with Robux cannot be sold for Bucks, even if you got it in a trade. It can still be traded to another player.

The steps, one by one

  1. The seller opens their mailbox and chooses to trade the house. A FOR TRADE sign appears at the house.
  2. The buyer walks in and tours the house.
  3. The buyer sends the trade request from inside the house.
  4. Both players accept. If either player changes anything, the trade is cancelled and you start again.

The furniture inside goes with the house, but it has no Bucks value, so selling it gives you nothing. Pet displays do not go with it. A house cannot be put on a trading stand. And the game does not keep a record of who owned a house before, which is one reason this site has a Build Registry.

Which Houses You Can Still Get

Not every house can be bought anymore. Most of them are bought with Bucks from the Build House menu, and a few are bought with Robux. Some have left the game and can only be traded for. As of October 2026, these houses are trade-only.

Seven left the shop one by one in the Last Chance House Sale, from June 8 to June 14, 2024: Biodome Home, Cozy Cabin, Eco Natural Earth House, Mansion, Mermaid Mansion, Pirate Ship House, Ruler's Castle.

Six more were part of Adopt Me's final-chance Robux sale on April 24, 2026, and are no longer sold: Celebrity Mansion, Expandable Dino House, Hospital Home, Modern Mansion, Schoolhouse Home, Soccer Stadium.

The Christmas Pudding House was a free reward in the 2025 Winter Event. It left the game on January 5, 2026.

Prices and availability can change with updates, so check in game before you plan a trade around a house. You can see the price and trade status of every house on the houses page, and new releases and removals are listed in the house news on the home page.

How the Price Gets Decided

Adopt Me does not set house prices, and the game does not tell you what a house is worth. Players agree on a price between themselves. Many listings on this site ask for a value in Shark, Frost or Ride Pot. This site never converts one of those into another, because there is no fixed rate between them.

Two houses of the same type can be very different. The decorations, the furniture and the theme all change what someone is willing to pay. That is why listings here have photos. It is also why the original or cloned question matters.

This site gives you two things to compare against. Each house page shows the asking prices from live listings, once at least three listings use the same unit. Asking prices are what people want, not what houses sold for. The Recent Trades page shows trades that both players confirmed after they happened, which is the closest thing to what really changed hands.

Tips for a Safer Trade

1. Tour the house before you accept

The buyer walks through the house before sending the request. Look at every room. Check that the furniture and the build match the photos and what the seller told you.

2. Check the build history

The game does not record previous owners. If you are paying extra for a special build, look it up in the Build Registry to see who built it and when.

3. Read everything before you accept

Look at both sides of the trade window before you press accept. If anything changes, the game cancels the trade, so check again every time.

4. Take your time

Nobody can make you hurry. A good trader will wait while you look things over.

5. Look at who you are trading with

On this site, a profile with real listings and confirmed trades tells you more than a brand new account with nothing on it.

6. Keep it in the game

Offers on this site only use items from Adopt Me. Never trade for Robux, gift cards or real money, and never share personal information like your real name, address or other accounts.

Red Flags to Watch Out For

Pressure to hurry. Lines like "quick, I'm about to leave" or "someone else just offered more, decide now" are tricks. Slow down or walk away.

A last-second change. A scam can show you one thing and swap it at the last moment, hoping you accept out of habit. Always read the final screen.

Anyone asking for Robux, gift cards or money. Trades here are for in-game items only. Roblox's rules also do not allow buying and selling items for real money outside the game.

An offer that is way too good, followed by a favor. If someone overpays for no reason and then says it was a mistake and asks you to give things back, stop and do not send anything.

Anyone asking for your password or personal details. A real trader never needs them.

If a trade feels off, trust that feeling and stop. There will always be another house to trade.

Rules in this guide come from Adopt Me's official House Trading FAQ and update notes, checked October 30, 2026. The game can change them, so check the help center if something looks different in game.
