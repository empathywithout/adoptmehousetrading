Adopt Me House News

A running list of when Adopt Me added, changed or removed houses. Entries come from Adopt Me's own update posts and help center, and from the Adopt Me Wiki. Last checked October 30, 2026.

Looking for a quick list instead? See which houses are trade-only or every house by release date.

2026

  • September 25, 2026

    Monster Mansion released

    The Monster Mansion came out for 13,000 Bucks. It has four levels, and its ten matching furniture pieces are sold separately.

    Houses: Monster Mansion

  • September 4, 2026

    Fairytale Castle released

    The Fairytale Castle came out for 8,000 Bucks. It is a four-story house, and its ten matching furniture pieces are sold separately.

    Houses: Fairytale Castle

  • July 24, 2026

    Beehive House released

    The Beehive House arrived with the Pollinator Season update for 5,450 Bucks. It is not a Bee Pass reward.

    Houses: Beehive House

  • April 24, 2026

    Final-chance Robux sale

    Adopt Me ran a final-chance Robux sale that included the Modern Mansion, Celebrity Mansion, Soccer Stadium, the School and Hospital bundle and the Fossil Isle bundle. Those houses are no longer sold and can only be traded for.

    Houses: Modern Mansion, Celebrity Mansion, Soccer Stadium, Hospital Home, Schoolhouse Home, Expandable Dino House

2025

  • December 5, 2025

    Christmas Pudding House event reward

    The Christmas Pudding House was a free reward for completing 15 pet needs in the Winter Event. It left the game on January 5, 2026, and can only be traded for now.

    Houses: Christmas Pudding House

  • September 26, 2025

    Halloween Hotel released

    The Halloween Hotel came out for 7,000 Bucks with the Halloween House update. It has three levels: a ground floor, a second floor and a basement.

    Houses: Halloween Hotel

  • May 9, 2025

    Moon House released

    The Moon House came out for 4,000 Bucks. It is a single room of rocky terrain with a portal, and a red button inside switches low gravity on and off.

    Houses: Moon House

  • February 28, 2025

    Ice Cream House released

    The Ice Cream House came out for 2,150 Bucks with a set of ice cream furniture. It has two floors.

    Houses: Ice Cream House

2024

  • July 12, 2024

    Soccer Stadium released

    The Soccer Stadium came out in the Soccer update as a Robux house with two floors and seven rooms. It was part of the April 2026 final-chance Robux sale, and can only be traded for now.

    Houses: Soccer Stadium

  • June 7, 2024

    Last Chance House Sale

    Seven houses went on sale and were removed one per day from June 8 to June 14, 2024: Mansion, Cozy Cabin, Biodome Home, Ruler's Castle, Pirate Ship House, Eco Natural Earth House and Mermaid Mansion.

    Houses: Mansion, Cozy Cabin, Biodome Home, Ruler's Castle, Pirate Ship House, Eco Natural Earth House, Mermaid Mansion

  • May 31, 2024

    House trading added

    Adopt Me added house trading on May 31, 2024. A player needs a trade license and at least two houses, and a house can be traded for anything except another house.

  • May 3, 2024

    Expandable Dino House released

    The Expandable Dino House came with the Fossil Isle Returns Bundle during Fossil Isle Excavation, along with the Legendary Prehistoric Car. It was part of the April 2026 final-chance Robux sale.

    Houses: Expandable Dino House

  • February 15, 2024

    Korean House released

    The Korean House came out in the second week of the Lunar New Year event for 2,200 Bucks.

    Houses: Korean House

  • January 11, 2024

    Queenslander House released

    The Queenslander House came out for 1,650 Bucks, with a matching furniture pack sold separately.

    Houses: Queenslander House

2023

  • August 31, 2023

    Expandable houses added

    Adopt Me added its first three expandable houses, each with a furniture pack. Expansions are bought separately with Bucks, and future houses may come with the feature.

    Houses: Expandable Friendly Home, Expandable Hillside Mansion, Expandable Luxury Mansion
