Adopt Me Build Registry — Protect Your Original Builds

+ Register a build

Builders spend thousands of Bucks and hours crafting unique houses, only to have cloners copy the design and sell it as their own. When the same build floods the market, original builders lose credit and traders get scammed paying original prices for dupes. The Build Registry is a public, timestamped record of who built what first. Register your build in seconds — if someone disputes your claim, the timestamp wins, but real proof of ownership like early screenshots or videos can override it. Builds go Verified after 30 days with no dispute, or get officially marked Original or Cloned after a real review. Know what you're trading for. Builders get the credit they're owed.

How the registry works

STEP 1
Register your build

Add photos of the house and say which house type it is. The entry gets a timestamp, and it is public.

STEP 2
Other people can look

Anyone can open the entry. People can heart builds they like, and the most hearted builds are shown at the top.

STEP 3
No dispute for 30 days

If nobody disputes it for 30 days, the entry is shown as verified by the community.

STEP 4
A dispute opens a review

If someone says they built it first, they send their claim and proof. You get one reply, and a reviewer on this site decides.

What the badges mean

  • Verified by the community: the entry is more than 30 days old and nobody has ever disputed it. It means nobody has challenged it, not that a reviewer checked it.
  • Original or Cloned: a reviewer looked at a dispute and made a ruling. Once it is decided, both sides of the dispute are shown publicly.
  • Disputed: someone has challenged the entry and the review is not finished yet.

What counts as proof

The timestamp counts first, but it can be overruled by real evidence. Early screenshots or video of the build going up, pictures taken before the other copy existed, and anything else that shows who made it first all help. Keep your progress photos, because they are the strongest proof you can have.

Why a registry at all

Adopt Me does not keep a record of who built a house or who owned it before. The game's own house trading rules say that no ownership history is recorded. So when a build is copied, there is nothing in the game that shows the original. This registry is a record kept by this fan site. It is not an official Adopt Me record, and it only shows what people have claimed and how disputes were decided.

Before you pay extra for a build

Look the build up here, and ask the seller for photos and for the build history. Read our guides on what a cloned house is and on original, cloned and commissioned builds so you know which questions to ask.
