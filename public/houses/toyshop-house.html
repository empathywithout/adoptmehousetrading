About the Toyshop House

Toyshop House came in the Aviator Kiwi and Toy Shop update in September 2023. You buy it with Robux, or get it by trading. The Kiwi, Red Scarf and a toyshop furniture set came out at the same time.

The house has 2 floors and 9 rooms. Six are on the first floor, joined by a narrow hallway. Three are on the second floor, joined by a small balcony. The walls are brown and peach with red and blue accents, including the roof. The windows are curved and yellow. Two small red gifts with glowing yellow bows sit outside.

What to look for in the Toyshop House

  • Brown and peach walls with red and blue accents including the roof
  • Curved yellow windows
  • Two small red gifts with glowing yellow bows on the exterior

Trading the Toyshop House

It is bought with Robux, so it cannot be resold for Bucks. Reports differ on the Robux price, so check it in game.

Every house trade follows the same official rules, including needing a trade license and owning at least two houses. The how to trade houses guide walks through them step by step.
