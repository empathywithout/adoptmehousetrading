About the Dragon's Castle

Dragon's Castle costs 3,000 Bucks and is bought through the Change Home menu. A release date has not been found. It is listed as available to buy, and no removal has been noted. It is a Bucks house, not a Robux one.

The house is shaped like a castle, with a windmill attached to it. A dragon structure sits on the roof, and its head follows the player. A door leads out to a balcony. Inside there are two floors and four rooms in total. No furniture set for this house has been found.

What to look for in the Dragon's Castle

  • Castle shape with attached windmill
  • Dragon structure on roof whose head follows the player
  • Door to a balcony

Trading the Dragon's Castle

It is still listed as a Bucks purchase, so you can get it from the shop instead of trading for it.
