About the Hospital Home

The Hospital Home came from the School and Hospital Homes gamepass, which covered both houses with one purchase. It arrived in the Hospital and School Refresh update on May 18, 2023. The bundle was part of Adopt Me's final-chance Robux sale on April 24, 2026 and is no longer sold. You can only get it by trading with another player.

The update also brought a set of hospital furniture, including beds, reception desks and cribs. The Schoolhouse Home came in the same pass.

What to look for in the Hospital Home

  • Hospital-themed furniture available: beds, reception desks, cribs

Trading the Hospital Home

It was bought with Robux, so it cannot be sold for Bucks, even if you got it in a trade.

Every house trade follows the same official rules, including needing a trade license and owning at least two houses. The how to trade houses guide walks through them step by step.
