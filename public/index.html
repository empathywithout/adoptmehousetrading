How house trading works here

Read the full guide →
STEP 1
List your house

Add at least five photos, say which house it is, and mark whether the build is original or cloned. Ask for a value in Shark, Frost or Ride Pot.

STEP 2
Get offers

Players offer pets, vehicles, toys and more. A house can be traded for anything except another house, and there is no Robux or real money in an offer.

STEP 3
Chat with preset messages

Trade chat uses ready-made messages only, so nobody has to type personal details to a stranger.

STEP 4
Confirm it

After the swap in game, both traders confirm it here. Trades confirmed by both sides show up on the Recent Trades page.
