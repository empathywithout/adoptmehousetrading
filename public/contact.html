Contact

The fastest way to reach us is through Discord. Almost everything happens there.

Join our Discord

Whether you need help with a listing, have a question about the site, want to report an issue, or just want to hang out with other house traders, Discord is the place to be.

Join our Discord

What can I get help with?

  • Questions about listings or offers
  • Reporting a scammer or bad actor
  • Bug reports or site feedback
  • Builder commission inquiries
  • Applying to the data team
  • Something on a house page that looks wrong
  • General trade advice

To get a fast answer

Tell us what happened and where. It helps to include:

  • The link to the listing, the build or the page
  • Your display name on the site
  • A screenshot, if you have one

Never send your password to anyone, including us. We will never ask for it.

Reporting a listing

Every listing has a "Report this listing" link. Use it for things like cross-trading, proxy trading, a build that is not what it says it is, or someone who did not show up. Reports and the Community Rules work together, so it is worth reading the rules first.

Things we cannot do

  • We cannot get back items or Robux lost in a trade. This site never holds items, and trades happen in the game.
  • We cannot take any action inside Adopt Me. For problems in the game or with your Roblox account, use Roblox and Adopt Me's own help pages.

Forgot your password?

If you added an email when you signed up, use the password reset page. If you did not add one, ask in Discord and we will see what we can do.
