About Us

A trading platform built by the Adopt Me community, for the Adopt Me community.

What is AdoptMeHouseTrading.com?

AdoptMeHouseTrading.com is a fan-made platform dedicated to Adopt Me house trading. We built it because house trading had no real home online. There was no good place to list your house, find serious offers, verify who built a build, or see what houses have actually traded for.

This site is that place. You can list your house, make offers, commission a builder, browse the build registry, and see what similar houses have actually traded for. Everything is designed around making trades safer, faster, and more fair.

Real trades, not guesses

Most value lists in the Adopt Me space come from one person's or one team's opinion. This site shows what is really happening on it instead. Each house page shows the asking prices from live listings, and the Recent Trades page shows trades that both traders confirmed after they happened.

A community Data Team can also log trades they have seen. We are still building this out, and if you trade regularly and want to help, you can apply to join the data team.

The Build Registry

The build registry is one of our most unique features. Builders can register their original builds with timestamped proof, creating a permanent record of who built what and when. It helps protect original creators and gives traders a way to verify a build's history before committing to a trade.

How we check our facts

The prices, release dates and trade rules on the house pages and in the guides come from Adopt Me's own update posts and help center, and from the Adopt Me Wiki. When sources disagree, we leave the number out instead of guessing. Each house page says when its facts were last checked, and the House Trading rules in our guide are taken from the official help center.

The game changes often, so something can be out of date. If you spot a mistake, tell us on Discord and we will check it.
